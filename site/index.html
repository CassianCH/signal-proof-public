How it works

Each signal is hashed with a random nonce, linked to the previous receipt, signed with Ed25519 and independently timestamped by an RFC 3161 TSA. Signed commitments are archived before disclosure; signal contents are released after 168 hours. Verification checks the disclosed content against its commitment, the signatures, timestamps and chain continuity.

TSA issuance within 15 minutes of receipt is classified as timely, with up to 5 minutes of clock tolerance for earlier timestamps. Later timestamps are marked late and retained in the archive and hash chain. Timing is assessed separately from cryptographic validity.
